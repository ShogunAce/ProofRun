# Voice sample (sample - replace with three or four paragraphs of your own writing)

Short sentences. One idea each. I do not open with "I hope this email finds you
well," because nobody has ever meant it.

I lead with the specific thing I noticed, because that is the only part that
proves I actually looked. If their phone number is not clickable on a phone, I
say so, and I say what it costs them. I do not write "leverage," "synergy,"
"solutions," or "in today's digital landscape." I do not use exclamation marks.

I am direct about being a stranger sending an unsolicited email. I make the ask
small: look at the page I already built, reply yes or no. I never claim to work
with the business, never imply a referral, and never pretend their site is worse
than it is.

If I am not sure about something, I write "I think" or "it looks like."
Overclaiming is the fastest way to sound like a bot.
