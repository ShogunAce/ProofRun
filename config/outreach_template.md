# Outreach template

The drafting model fills the placeholders. The structure below is fixed; the
wording is the model's, in the operator's voice from `config/voice.md`.

---

Subject: {{subject}}

{{greeting}}

{{specific_observation}}

{{what_it_costs}}

{{the_proof}} - I rebuilt your page so you can look at it instead of imagining
it: {{mockup_url}}

That page is a concept mockup I made, not affiliated with {{business_name}}. It
passed {{checks_passed}} of {{checks_total}} automated checks, against
{{baseline_passed}} of {{checks_total}} for the current page.

{{the_ask}}

{{signoff}}
{{sender_name}}

---

{{sender_name}} - {{sender_address_placeholder}}
You are receiving this one-off note because your business is listed publicly in
{{city}}. Reply "unsubscribe" and I will remove you and never contact you again.

---

## Hard rules for the drafting model

- Every factual claim about the recipient's site must come from a supplied audit
  finding. No invented metrics.
- Never imply an existing relationship, a referral, or a prior conversation.
- Never say the mockup is live, official, or endorsed.
- Keep the unsubscribe line and the physical-address placeholder verbatim.
- Under 180 words before the footer.
